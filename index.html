Returns & Warranty

×

Everything I sell is used/vintage unless a listing says otherwise, and I describe each item's condition as accurately as I can. Because these are pre-owned collectibles, here's what to expect:

  • Local pickup: Please look over the item before you pay — once you've paid and taken it home, the sale is final.
  • Consoles, games, and electronics: These are tested before listing, but occasionally something doesn't show up right away (a disc that skips, a console issue that wasn't apparent in testing). If that happens, message me within 7 days of pickup/delivery and I'll work with you on a repair, replacement, or partial refund.
  • Shipped items: If something arrives significantly different than described, or was damaged in transit, message me within 3 days of delivery and I'll make it right.
  • No returns for buyer's remorse, or once an item's been used beyond what's needed to check it works.
  • All returns and refunds are at my discretion. I want to be fair, but I reserve the right to decline a request if I feel the situation doesn't warrant one.
